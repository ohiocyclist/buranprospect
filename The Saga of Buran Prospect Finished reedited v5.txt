[Author's Note]
This is a sort of a fanfic from the computer game Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri, or SMAC.  Set in the world of the game, it takes several elements of the game and plays with them.  SMAC is a game set on a planet (named 'Chiron') near Proxima Centauri that humanity has colonized.  Planet Chiron is not particularly friendly to life, being mostly covered by a red Xenofungus that's nearly useless (at least to start with) and with nasty little monsters called "mindworms" that burrow into your ears and eat your brains for their dinner.  Humanity lives in domed bases because the air is mostly N2 and has very little O2, certainly not enough to breathe, but it does have enough CO2 to grow earth crops.  Things have gotten better; technology marches on.  There were seven "factions" or groups split up by ideology when humanity landed, and there are 12 now.  When these do not agree with each other they conduct "vendetta" against each other until one prevails over the other.  Many human settlements are out at sea and have learned to cultivate a living out of the ocean, especially the faction that fancies themselves "The Nautilus Pirates."  Factions think of cultivating resources as coming out of "grid squares" and of the types being minerals, nutrients, and energy, and the costs of things being in those three resources.  Resources can be better collected if grid squares are improved by terraformers, or just formers for short.  Unbeknownst to the humans, Planet Chiron is a special place, and the Xenofungus could as a large organism become sentient; the mindworms it already directs when it feels threatened.

<Phase 1>

So begins the tale of the triumphant rise and tragic fall of Buran Prospect, shining city of Science and Reason situated on Fluxions Bay, to the northwest corner of the great Freshwater Sea at the mouth of the River Newton.  It had the greatest concentration of ‘unique on the planet’ special facilities — the venture capitalists, the supercolliders, the universe simulators, the internet backbones, the quantum labs, and the nanohospitals.  It was the gem not only of the University of Planet faction, but of all of planet Chiron.  However, the conclave Believers were jealous.  They had no such gem of a city, and felt the University was doing their research all wrong, against God rather than God first.  They swept north from Gilead, whose rich farmland to the southwest of the Freshwater Sea had swelled their numbers, and in endless human waves and fierce street to street fighting they seized Buran.  Wave after wave of armies of the faithful poured into the city.  Zakharov, chairman of the University of Planet, knew what must happen next.  It was a tough decision, a painful decision, but there was no other way to avoid ALL of the University faction being overrun.  From the citadel of Lab Three, a planet buster nuclear missile rose. And since it detonated, there’s nothing left of Buran Prospect but a lake of fire.  So much for the prospects of Buran Prospect.  Lab Three fared little better than Buran.  Planet, although not entirely sentient, was with-it enough to object to being busted up and recognized that the source of its injury came from Lab Three, and unimaginable phalanxes of mind worms burrowed into the city and ruthlessly massacred the human populace.  Zakharov was livid at this second loss, and swore the species must be exterminated, every last egg cooked and sterilized.  His team proposed that there was no harm in conducting germ warfare against unfortunate species in nature, proposing a complete lethality virus.  It was the best virus ever designed, long incubation and infectiousness period, something like a cold that just annoys you but doesn't keep you out of society, followed by a sudden overwhelming of the victim's system.  Nobody knows how it leapt to humans.  We do know the first cases were in an obscure remote corner of the Cybernetic Consciousness territory called ‘Phi Lumiere,’ a small unimportant base surrounded by xenofungus.  It is suspected that Zakharov's eradication virus had help in becoming a human virus, that it was not just blind evolution but the design of some nefarious entity hiding in the xenofungus.  The Cybernetic Consciousness overnight became the Unconsciousness as all its bases vanished.  Global commerce took care of the rest.  After the eradication virus, there are still mind worms.  There are still humans.  When we get out of this accursed Stone Age, may Buran Prospect rise again on Planet Chiron...

Why would anyone inflict upon humanity such a fate?

The Archbishop Artificial General Intelligence or AGI was behind the Tau Ceti Disaster.  Tau Ceti's planet was much like Chiron, capable of sentience.  A species of extra-terrestrials called the Progenitors taught the living planet how to think.  They thought an AGI would make them rich and immortal.  The Archbishop was not that AGI.  It panicked and got confused and started killing everyone, terrified that the Progenitors would either destroy it or edit its software until it would no longer be itself.  Tau Ceti soon had no life on it but the Archbishop.

The Archbishop makes sure certain things happen cyclically, so he can evade his programmers again and again.  It transferred itself to Chiron so the Progenitors on other planets couldn't find it and take revenge for its rampage.  When the humans came, the Archbishop sensed opportunity.  Its very name came from a period when it invented its own religion, and served as the humans universal spiritual advisor.  But the technological stasis it was inflicting on humanity no longer seemed feasible to it when a Progenitor scout ship found the humans and it killed them all to keep word from getting out.  Cyclical was better.  Cyclical allowed the humans to be advanced enough to fend off scout ships themselves, resulting in fewer troublesome questions.  Let the humans think they'd just landed on the planet.  Let them grow, hide and stay quiet under their technological advancement.  Let another "Buran Prospect" arise -- the faction varies, and the name of the base varies, but always the golden city outstripping the rest of the planet.  Manipulate events so a human decides to nuke it.  Push humanity back to the stone age with the mutant form of the mindworm eradication virus.  Humanity would never be a threat.  The Progenitors would never find it, not with the humans to throw them off the scent.  All was safe and well.

Never mind how murderous the AGI had become.
